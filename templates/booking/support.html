Frequently asked questions

How do I find a bus? Search by route, travel date, bus type, or operator on the home page. You can filter for trips with available seats.

Can I choose my seat? Yes. Open a trip to see its seat map, including decks and sleeper berths where available. Grey places are unavailable.

How long is a seat held? Ten minutes. Confirm within that time or choose available seats again.

What are the extra charges? The bill shows the seat fare, platform fee, service fee, and illustrative GST on the fees before confirmation. All payments are simulated.

Can I choose where to board? Yes. Select the boarding and drop-off points while reserving a seat. Your ticket shows both points and their scheduled times.

What happens if my trip is full? Join its waiting list. When a place opens, the next person in line receives a time-limited offer in My bookings.

What happens after cancellation? The place returns to inventory or is offered to the next waiting passenger. A confirmed demo payment receives a simulated full refund.

Where is my ticket? Open My bookings and select a confirmed journey to view or print it.

When can I rate a bus? After a confirmed journey has arrived, you can leave one review from its booking page.

How do I get more help? Send a request using the form above. Its status and any staff response appear on this page.
